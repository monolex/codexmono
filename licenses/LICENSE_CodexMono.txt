CODEXMONO PHILOSOPHY & SUFFIX USAGE:
While "CodexMono™" is a trademark, we encourage the community to use
"CodexMono" as a SUFFIX to indicate adherence to strict monospace principles.

PERMITTED SUFFIX USAGE:
✓ [Font Name] CodexMono (e.g., "Noto Sans CodexMono", "Pretendard CodexMono")

When you use "CodexMono" as a suffix, you commit to:
1. Maintaining strict fixed-width character cells
2. No variable spacing or proportional adjustments
3. Consistent baseline and metrics across all glyphs
4. Code-first design philosophy
